Heuristic:
For my heuristic, I used a version of hill climbing algorithm. Because the Heuristic function needs to give the 
worse state, I would count the amount of blocks that is out of place or has a block underneath it out of place. 
I would basically count up from the bottom of stack and once the stack stops matching I would count every block compared
to the goal state 

I also would also reward states for having consecutive blocks in place which is probably why my B19 problem was
slow as it only has two stacks in the goal state. For every block that is on top of its correct block with no blocks 
under it out of place I would subtract two. 

The function that uses this heuristic is called hill_climbing()

This gives a heuristic of -3 as missing B on top of G in stack two, B, A, C, and F all add +1 but the E, H, I, J all give -2 resulting in -3
